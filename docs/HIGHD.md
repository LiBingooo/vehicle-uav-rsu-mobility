# Using highD

[highD (Highway Drone Dataset)](https://levelxdata.com/highd-dataset/) provides real vehicle trajectories recorded by drones over German highways.

## Apply and Download

1. Visit the [official dataset page](https://levelxdata.com/highd-dataset/).
2. Find **Application for Access for Non-Commercial Use**.
3. Provide your name, institutional affiliation, official email address, and project use; accept the terms and submit.
4. Requests are reviewed manually. After approval, follow the provider's instructions to obtain the files.
5. Keep your downloaded files under `data/private/`.

The provider controls access and permitted use. This repository does not redistribute the dataset.

## Recording Files

For recording `01`, use:

- `01_tracks.csv`: vehicle trajectory observations.
- `01_recordingMeta.csv`: recording metadata, including `frameRate`.

Use the frame rate in your recording metadata. For a 25 fps recording:

```bash
python scripts/preview.py highd --tracks data/private/01_tracks.csv --fps 25 --start 0 --duration 40 --view all --output output/private
```

## Coordinate Handling

The reader uses `frame,id,x,y,xVelocity`. Frame 1 corresponds to time zero. Positions remain in metres; `--x-origin` and `--y-origin` optionally translate the coordinate origin. Time is rebased to the first retained observation.

The adapter preserves observations without interpolation, road tiling, or vehicle duplication. Missing observation intervals are skipped. Vehicles outside the configured road bounds are not drawn. UAVs and RSUs are generated by the scene model.

Local exports may contain dataset information and remain subject to the [highD terms](https://levelxdata.com/highd-dataset/). Cite the dataset as requested by its provider when using it in research.
